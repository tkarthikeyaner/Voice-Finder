# Voice Finder 1.3.0

**New: Battery saver**
- **Pause when battery is low:** pick a level from 10% to 50% (default 20%). Listening resumes by itself 3% above that level, or as soon as you plug in.
- **Pause while charging:** resumes by itself when you unplug.
- While paused, the mic and wake lock are fully off, so no battery is spent listening. The notification and the app show "Paused" and why.

**New: Custom "found" banner**
- Set your own **title** and **message** for the screen that appears when your phone is found (also used in the notification).
- Choose from 5 colour themes, with a live preview in the app. **Reset to default** brings back the original.

Both are off or unchanged by default; turn them on in the new **Battery saver** and **Found screen banner** cards.

**Install:** download `VoiceFinder-1.3.0.apk` below and install it over the old version. Your voice profile and settings are kept.
